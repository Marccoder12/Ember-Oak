Our Story

Ember & Oak began with a simple idea: bring people together over food made with fire, patience, and good ingredients. Inspired by the warmth of an open flame and the timeless character of oak, we create dishes that feel familiar while still giving you something worth remembering. From the first spark in the kitchen to the last bite at the table, everything we serve is made with care. This is a place to slow down, share a meal, and stay a little longer.
